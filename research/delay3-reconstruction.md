# Aka Delay3 routines

`aka-jp-rev0` is the Japanese origin reference. All 7 cataloged releases (Japanese, German, English, Spanish, French, Italian) contain one `Delay3` tail-call bound to the previously verified release-specific `DelayFrames` address and one `ProtectedDelay3` wrapper that preserves BC. Reports retain identities, call relationships and hashes without ROM bytes; release status is not promoted.
